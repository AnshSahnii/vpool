How it works

  1. Register and complete your profile (vehicle details for drivers).
  2. Drivers offer rides with pickup, destination, time & seats.
  3. Passengers search or get matched to rides on their route.
  4. Join a ride instantly or send a request the driver can accept.
  5. Track everything from your Driver / Passenger dashboard.
